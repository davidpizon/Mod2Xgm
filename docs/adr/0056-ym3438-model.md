# ADR 0056: YM3438 model: a bit-exact ymfm port, checked against Nuked-OPN2 (T7.2)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-30

## Context

Plan task T7.2: the optimizer has to hear what its FM candidates sound like on a Genesis, inside the converter, with
no native library for it ([ADR 0010](0010-multi-pass-optimizer-sgdk-compliance-pure-csharp.md)). The plan named a C#
port of ymfm (BSD-3) and asked for two checks: the port matches ymfm's output, and it holds up against an independent
model ("BlastEm captures").

The Genesis shipped with two versions of the chip. The discrete YM2612 (Model 1) has the "ladder effect": an extra step
around zero that makes quiet sounds crunchy and leaks muted channels. The CMOS YM3438 (many Model 2s) doesn't. ymfm
models both.

BlastEm 0.6.2 can't write its audio to a file, and recording it by hand was ruled out (David).

## Decision

- **Model the YM3438, the clean chip, only** (David). `src/Render/Fm/` (`Ym3438`, `FmEngine`, `FmChannel`,
  `FmOperator`, `OpnRegisters`, `FmTables`) is a port of ymfm's `ym3438` at commit `81aec25` (`src/ymfm_opn.cpp`,
  `src/ymfm_fm.ipp`, `src/ymfm.h`), cut down to the OPN2: six 4-operator channels, OPNA register layout, the LFO,
  SSG-EG, CH3 special mode and the 9-bit DAC. The OPL, OPM and OPLL paths, the timers, the status register, IRQs, the
  busy flag and CSM mode are left out: nothing that renders a song needs them, and SGDK's drivers don't use CSM.
- **The port is bit-exact with ymfm, mistakes included.** Where ymfm differs from the chip, the port does too, and the
  difference is recorded below. Fixing those is plan T7.7 (David): one at a time, each measured.
- **The output is ymfm's:** one stereo sample of integers per 144 master clocks (53 267 Hz on NTSC), the sum of the
  six channels' 9-bit values × 128 / 6, so full scale is about ±32 768. Writes take effect before the next sample.
  Resampling and mixing with the PSG and PCM are for the Genesis renderer (T7.5).
- **Nuked-OPN2 takes BlastEm's place as the independent check** (David). Nuked-OPN2 (LGPL-2.1, commit `335747d`) is a
  cycle-accurate model of the YM3438 built from its die shot, so it stands in for the real chip. It is used only by a
  test tool, never shipped, and none of its code is copied into the converter.
- **Reference outputs are made once and checked in** (David). `tools/ym3438-reference/run.sh` downloads ymfm and
  Nuked-OPN2 at the pinned commits, builds `reference.cpp` with g++, plays every probe script in
  `tests/golden/ym3438/` through both, and writes `<probe>.ymfm.gz` and `<probe>.nuked.gz` (gzipped little-endian int32
  pairs, 0.6 MB for all eleven probes). CI needs no C++ compiler.
- **Probe scripts are text** (`w part reg value`, `s samples`), built in code by `Ym3438Probes` and kept current by a
  test, so the tool and the tests play the same writes. Eleven probes cover the algorithms, feedback, envelopes and key
  scaling, detune and multiple, the LFO, SSG-EG, CH3 special mode, the DAC, all six channels clipping, and write quirks
  (the F-number latch, mid-note changes, partial key-ons, the missing channel 3).
- **Nuked's writes take time and ymfm's don't**, so the tool schedules each run of writes to end on the sample
  boundary where the script puts it (setup writes run before the first sample, with their output discarded), gives a
  key-on 24 clocks to reach its channel's slot, and starts on a whole period of Nuked's envelope timer so both models'
  envelope counters agree. Nuked's output pins are summed over each sample's 24 clocks; each channel is on the pins for
  18 of them, so ×128/18 matches ymfm's scale. Its pipeline puts it 3–4 samples behind ymfm.

## The checks

| Test | Bar |
|---|---|
| Port vs ymfm, every probe | Identical, sample for sample (it is) |
| Port vs Nuked-OPN2, log-mel distance at 53 267 Hz | 0.6 dB, or a per-probe bar for a known difference (below) |

Measured against Nuked-OPN2 when the bars were set:

| Probe | dB | Bar |
|---|---|---|
| envelopes | 0.03 | 0.6 |
| sine | 0.04 | 0.6 |
| dac | 0.13 | 0.6 |
| feedback | 0.40 | 0.6 |
| lfo | 0.52 | 0.6 |
| algorithms | 0.88 | 1.0, known difference 3 |
| ch3-special | 0.95 | 1.2, known difference 1 |
| write-quirks | 1.12 | 1.5, known difference 3 |
| detune-multiple | 2.76 | 3.0, known differences 3 and 4 |
| ssg-eg | 2.95 | 3.5, known difference 2 |
| six-channels | 3.52 | 4.0, known difference 1 |

A single operator matches Nuked-OPN2 to 1 LSB at every detune and multiple, and the envelope probe to 0.03 dB.

## Known differences from the chip

Found by comparing ymfm with Nuked-OPN2. Each is in ymfm, so it is in the port; T7.7 closes them.

1. **Clipping order.** The chip adds a channel's operators in slot order (O1, O3, O2, O4) and clips to −256…255 after
   each one. ymfm starts from O4, adds O1, O2, O3 and clips to −257…256. When several loud carriers saturate, the
   results differ: about 3 dB less level on the chip with four full-level carriers (six-channels).
2. **SSG-EG alternating shapes (2 and 6).** At each turn the chip outputs a brief full-level blip, a few samples long;
   ymfm turns smoothly. Nuked has 20 dB more energy above 1 kHz on those shapes.
3. **Operators one slot at a time.** The chip handles operators one slot after another, so O3 is computed before O2
   and is modulated by the previous sample's O2, and each operator's key-on and phase reset lands at a slightly
   different moment. ymfm does every operator of a sample at once. It matters in long modulation chains (algorithms
   0–3) and when two carriers' phases interfere.
4. **Detune overflow at the top of the range** (block 7, F-number `$7FF`): ymfm keeps 17 bits of the phase step; the
   chip's result differs by about 1 dB on that note.

## Options considered

- **YM3438, bit-exact ymfm port, Nuked-OPN2 check (chosen).**
- **YM2612 too, as the default.** It's what a Model 1 sounds like, ladder effect and all; David chose the clean chip.
- **Fix the known differences in this task.** More accurate sooner, but a bigger change and no longer checkable
  against ymfm on those probes; moved to T7.7 so each fix is measured on its own.
- **Build ymfm in CI on every run.** Always fresh, but needs a C++ compiler on three OSes and makes CI slower and more
  fragile than comparing with checked-in files.
- **BlastEm captures.** Its vgmplay can't write a file; recording the speakers by hand or automatically (other sounds
  get in) was turned down. Building BlastEm's FM core from source would have needed a GPL-3 approval, and its core is
  partly based on the same research as Nuked's.
- **Gens.** An older, less exact FM core; it would mostly show Gens's own mistakes.

## Consequences

- `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` reproduces ymfm's BSD-3 notice (shipped) and lists Nuked-OPN2 (test tool only).
- Changing a probe means: `UPDATE_GOLDEN=1 dotnet test` to rewrite the scripts, then `tools/ym3438-reference/run.sh`.
- The port has no idea of time beyond samples: the Genesis renderer (T7.5) owns the clock, the write timing and
  resampling to the scoring rate.
- docs/genesis-audio.md's DAC flaw note is about the YM2612; the model won't show the ladder effect.
- New plan task T7.7 closes the four differences. Each fix leaves the port bit-exact with ymfm everywhere else and
  lowers that probe's Nuked bar.
